Post: Stop Asking for 30-Minute Meetings
Engagement: 215 Likes | 57 Comments | Date: April 28, 2026

"90% of B2B cold emails jump straight to a marriage proposal on the first touch: 'Can we book a 30-minute call this Wednesday?'

This is a Level 3 commitment ask, and it completely tanks response rates. No one wants to give 30 minutes of their day to an unverified stranger who just invaded their inbox.

If you want to triple your replies, shift your Call to Action (CTA) to a Level 1 request: 'Mind if I send you a quick 1-pager showing how we did this?' or 'Would you be open to a short visual breakdown of our AWS pipeline?'

You are offering an asset instead of demanding their time. Once they say 'Yes' and consume your value, moving them to a live call becomes natural. Sell the next step, not the final sale."
