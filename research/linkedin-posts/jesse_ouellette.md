Post: Why Your Open-Tracking Pixel is Killing Your Sales
Engagement: 142 Likes | 38 Comments | Date: May 12, 2026

"Your open rate should be zero. Why? Because tracking open rates is actively killing your email deliverability.

The industry is addicted to false positives. Every time you inject an open-tracking pixel or a custom click-tracking redirect, modern security proxies (like those used by Google and Microsoft Enterprise filters) automatically fetch and scan those URLs before delivering the mail.

This registers as a 'fake open' on your dashboard, making you think your campaign is crushing it. In reality, the security filter has just identified your sending infrastructure as an automated tracking source. Your chances of getting routed straight to the spam folder instantly multiply by 3x to 4x.

Turn tracking off. Send raw, un-tracked emails. Your open rates will look like zero on paper, but your actual responses and booked revenue will skyrocket because you are finally hitting the primary inbox."
